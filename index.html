ABOUT ME

I'm originally from Oregon and now based in Phoenix, Arizona for the past three years, I have always had a deep love for photography and storytelling. What began as a personal hobby has grown into a passion I'm excited to turn into a meaningful career through my upcoming business, Midnight Memory Co.

As both a photographer and writer, I believe the most meaningful memories deserve to be preserved in more than one way. Photography captures the emotion, connection, and beauty of a moment, while storytelling gives those memories a deeper voice and lasting meaning. Through thoughtfully captured images and custom-written stories, I hope to help clients celebrate the people, milestones, and experiences that matter most.

Midnight Memory Co. is built around creating timeless keepsakes that allow you to revisit the joy, love, and significance behind your special moments for years to come.

My Photography Philosophy

“

Photography is a blessing to me in a way to preserve the beauty, emotion, and meaning found in life's most sacred moments. From golden sunsets and the quiet wonder of nature to the genuine connection shared between loved ones, I am passionate about capturing the moments that deserve to be remembered.

Through my lens, I aim to tell authentic stories and create images that bring people back to how they felt in that exact moment. My goal is not simply to take photos, but to retain joy, love, beauty, and the memories that become more valuable with time.

Every photograph is an opportunity to turn a fleeting moment into something everlasting something you can hold onto, share, and cherish for a lifetime.

— Genavy Bartlett
